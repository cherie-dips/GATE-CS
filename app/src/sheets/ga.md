## Verbal Ability {#verbal-ability}

### Subject–verb agreement
- *Each, every, either, neither, everyone, anybody* → singular verb: "Each of the boys **is**"
- "One of the + plural noun" → singular verb: "One of the books **is** missing"; but "one of those who **are**" (verb agrees with *those*)
- *Either…or / neither…nor / not only…but also* → verb agrees with the **nearer** subject
- *A number of* + plural verb; *the number of* + singular verb
- *Along with, as well as, together with, in addition to* don't change the subject's number
- Collective nouns (team, committee) take singular verbs when acting as one unit
- *Amount* for uncountable, *number* for countable; *less* for uncountable, *fewer* for countable

### Tenses & conditionals
- If + present, will + verb (real future): "If it rains, I **will** stay"
- If + past, would + verb (unreal present): "If I **were** rich, I **would** travel"
- If + past perfect, would have + past participle (unreal past): "If I **had known**, I **would have** come"
- *Since* + point in time, *for* + duration (with present perfect)
- Reported speech shifts one tense back (is → was, will → would)

### Commonly confused words
| Pair | Meaning |

| affect / effect | verb (influence) / noun (result) |
| principal / principle | main, head / rule, belief |
| complement / compliment | completes / praise |
| stationary / stationery | not moving / paper, pens |
| lose / loose | misplace / not tight |
| its / it's | possessive / it is |
| who / whom | subject / object |
| between / among | two / more than two |
| imply / infer | speaker suggests / listener concludes |
| eminent / imminent | famous / about to happen |
| elicit / illicit | draw out / illegal |
| discreet / discrete | careful, tactful / separate |
| advice / advise | noun / verb |
| farther / further | physical distance / additional, figurative |

### Articles & prepositions
- *A* before consonant **sounds** (a university, a one-way), *an* before vowel sounds (an hour, an MBA)
- *The* for something specific or unique (the sun), superlatives (the best), ordinals (the first)
- Fixed pairs: *different from*, *comply with*, *prefer A to B*, *superior to*, *consist of*, *accused of*, *charged with*

### Reading comprehension & sentence ordering
- Answer only from the passage; reject options that are true in general but not stated or implied
- "Inference" = must follow from the passage; "assumption" = unstated premise the argument needs
- Sentence ordering: find the opening sentence (introduces the topic, no pronoun reference back), then link pronouns, connectives (*however, therefore, this*), and chronology

## Numerical / Quantitative Ability {#numerical-ability}

### Percentages
- $x\%$ of $y$ = $y\%$ of $x$
- Successive changes $a\%$ then $b\%$: net $a+b+\frac{ab}{100}\%$ (use negatives for decreases)
- Price up $r\%$: to keep expense the same, cut consumption by $\frac{r}{100+r}\times100\%$
- $A$ is $r\%$ more than $B$ ⇒ $B$ is $\frac r{100+r}\times100\%$ less than $A$
- Population growth: $P(1+\frac r{100})^n$; depreciation $P(1-\frac r{100})^n$

### Profit, loss, discount
- Profit% = $\frac{SP-CP}{CP}\times100$ (on cost price, unless told otherwise)
- Discount% = $\frac{MP-SP}{MP}\times100$ (on marked price)
- Successive discounts $a\%,b\%$ = single discount $a+b-\frac{ab}{100}\%$
- Selling two items at the same SP, one at $x\%$ profit and one at $x\%$ loss: net loss $\frac{x^2}{100}\%$

### Interest
- Simple: $SI=\frac{PRT}{100}$; compound: $A=P\left(1+\frac{R}{100}\right)^T$, $CI=A-P$
- Compounded $k$ times a year: $A=P\left(1+\frac R{100k}\right)^{kT}$
- CI − SI for 2 years $=P\left(\frac R{100}\right)^2$; for 3 years $=P\left(\frac R{100}\right)^2\left(3+\frac R{100}\right)$
- Doubling time ≈ $72/R$ years (rule of 72)

### Ratio, mixtures, averages
- $a:b$ and $b:c$ ⇒ scale to a common $b$; $\frac ab=\frac cd\Rightarrow\frac{a+b}{a-b}=\frac{c+d}{c-d}$
- Alligation: cheaper : dearer $=(d-m):(m-c)$ ($c$ = cheaper price, $d$ = dearer, $m$ = mean price)
- Replacement: $x$ litres removed from $V$ and replaced with water, $n$ times ⇒ pure left $=V\left(1-\frac xV\right)^n$
- Weighted average $=\frac{\sum w_ix_i}{\sum w_i}$; average of an AP = (first + last)/2
- Partnership: profit share ∝ capital × time

### Time & work, pipes
- Work = rate × time; $A$ alone in $a$ days, $B$ alone in $b$ days ⇒ together $\frac{ab}{a+b}$ days
- $\frac{M_1D_1H_1}{W_1}=\frac{M_2D_2H_2}{W_2}$ (men × days × hours per unit of work)
- Pipes: filling rates add, emptying rates subtract: net rate $\frac1a+\frac1b-\frac1c$

### Speed, distance, time
- km/h × $\frac5{18}$ = m/s; m/s × $\frac{18}5$ = km/h
- Average speed for equal distances at $x$ and $y$: $\frac{2xy}{x+y}$ (harmonic mean), not $\frac{x+y}2$
- Relative speed: same direction $|u-v|$, opposite $u+v$
- Train passing a pole: length / speed; passing a platform or another train: (sum of lengths) / relative speed
- Boats: downstream $u+v$, upstream $u-v$; still water $u=\frac{d+s}2$, stream $v=\frac{d-s}2$
- Meeting after starting together towards each other: time = distance / $(u+v)$
- Two bodies start towards each other and take $a$ and $b$ hours after meeting to finish: $S_1:S_2=\sqrt b:\sqrt a$

### Numbers
- Divisibility: 3 / 9 → digit sum; 4 → last two digits; 8 → last three; 11 → (alternate digit sums) difference divisible by 11; 7, 11, 13 → 1001
- $N=p^aq^br^c$: number of divisors $(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)$; sum of divisors $\frac{p^{a+1}-1}{p-1}\cdots$
- HCF × LCM = product (two numbers). HCF of fractions = HCF(numerators)/LCM(denominators); LCM of fractions = LCM(num)/HCF(den)
- Highest power of prime $p$ in $n!$: $\sum_k\lfloor n/p^k\rfloor$; trailing zeros of $n!$: $\sum_k\lfloor n/5^k\rfloor$
- Last digit cycles: 2, 3, 7, 8 → period 4; 4, 9 → period 2; 0, 1, 5, 6 → constant
- Fermat: $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$ ($p$ prime, $p\nmid a$); remainders multiply: $(ab)\bmod n=((a\bmod n)(b\bmod n))\bmod n$
- Digits of $N$: $\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor+1$
- $a^n-b^n$ is divisible by $a-b$ always, and by $a+b$ for even $n$; $a^n+b^n$ is divisible by $a+b$ for odd $n$
- Sums: $1+\cdots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}2$, squares $\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6$, cubes $\left(\frac{n(n+1)}2\right)^2$; first $n$ odd numbers $=n^2$

### Progressions & means
- AP: $a_n=a+(n-1)d$, $S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d]=\frac n2(a+l)$
- GP: $a_n=ar^{n-1}$, $S_n=a\frac{r^n-1}{r-1}$, $S_\infty=\frac a{1-r}$ ($|r|<1$)
- AM ≥ GM ≥ HM (equality iff all equal); two numbers: GM² = AM × HM

### Exponents & logarithms
- $a^ma^n=a^{m+n}$, $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$, $a^{-n}=1/a^n$, $a^0=1$
- $\log(xy)=\log x+\log y$, $\log\frac xy=\log x-\log y$, $\log x^n=n\log x$
- $\log_ab=\frac{\log b}{\log a}$, $\log_ab\cdot\log_ba=1$, $a^{\log_ax}=x$, $\log_{a^k}b=\frac1k\log_ab$

### Clocks & calendars
- Angle between hands at $H$:$M$ $=\left|30H-5.5M\right|$ (take $360°-\theta$ if $>180°$)
- Minute hand gains $5.5°$ per minute; hands coincide every $\frac{720}{11}=65\frac5{11}$ min
- In 12 hours: coincide 11 times, right angle 22 times, opposite 11 times (double for 24 h)
- Odd days: ordinary year 1, leap year 2; 100 years 5, 200 years 3, 300 years 1, 400 years 0
- Leap year: divisible by 4; century years only if divisible by 400

### Counting & probability (quick)
- See Engineering Mathematics → Probability and Discrete Mathematics → Combinatorics
- Handshakes among $n$ people: $\binom n2$; matches in a round robin: $\binom n2$; knockout: $n-1$

### Data interpretation
- Percentage change $=\frac{\text{new}-\text{old}}{\text{old}}\times100$; percentage point ≠ percent
- Growth rate over $n$ years (CAGR) $=\left(\frac{\text{final}}{\text{initial}}\right)^{1/n}-1$
- Read the axis units and whether values are cumulative before computing

## Analytical & Logical Reasoning {#logical-reasoning}

### Syllogisms
- "All A are B": A inside B. "Some A are B": overlap. "No A is B": disjoint. "Some A are not B": part of A outside B
- Draw the minimal Venn diagram satisfying all statements; a conclusion follows only if it is true in **every** valid diagram
- All + All → All; All + No → No; Some + All → Some; Some + No → Some-not; Some + Some → no definite conclusion
- "All A are B" does not give "All B are A"; it gives "Some B are A" (if A is non-empty)
- Either–or case: when neither "Some A are B" nor "No A is B" follows but they are complementary, the answer is "either"

### Arrangements & puzzles
- Linear: fix the most constrained person first; circular: fix one person to remove rotations
- Facing the centre in a circle: their left = clockwise
- Tabulate (rows = people, columns = attributes) and eliminate

### Directions & blood relations
- Right turn = 90° clockwise; facing north, right is east
- Shortest distance = $\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}$; track net east–west and north–south moves
- Blood relations: draw a family tree with gender marks; generations as levels

### Series & coding
- Look at differences (and differences of differences), ratios, squares/cubes ± k, primes, alternating series
- Letter positions A = 1 … Z = 26; reverse position = 27 − position; EJOTY marks 5, 10, 15, 20, 25

### Critical reasoning
- Statement–assumption: the assumption must be needed for the statement to make sense
- Statement–conclusion: must follow directly; ignore outside knowledge
- Cause–effect, strengthen / weaken: find the claim and the evidence first

## Spatial Aptitude {#spatial-aptitude}

### Cubes
- $n\times n\times n$ cube painted on all faces, cut into unit cubes: 3 faces painted $8$; 2 faces $12(n-2)$; 1 face $6(n-2)^2$; none $(n-2)^3$
- Dice: two positions sharing a face ⇒ the remaining faces in the same positions are opposite; adjacent faces are never opposite
- Standard die: opposite faces sum to 7

### Counting figures
- Squares in an $n\times n$ grid: $\sum_{k=1}^nk^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6$; rectangles (incl. squares) $\binom{n+1}2^2$
- $m\times n$ grid rectangles: $\binom{m+1}2\binom{n+1}2$
- Triangles in a triangle divided by lines from one vertex to $k$ points on the opposite side: $\binom{k+2}2$

### Transformations
- Mirror image: left–right swap (vertical mirror); water image: top–bottom swap
- Rotation by 90° clockwise: $(x,y)\to(y,-x)$; paper folding: unfold in reverse order, reflecting holes across each fold
